# Voice lounge — v0.6.0

The live lobby denied Speak, Voice Activity and Stream while allowing entry. Copying those overwrites made temporary rooms silent. New rooms inherit the parent category template, preserving category privacy and moderation restrictions. Legacy tracked rooms matching the lobby template are repaired on reconciliation. Manually changed or relocated rooms require staff review; unrelated channels are never deleted.

`/join-to-create` retains its existing enabled/hub/panel options and additionally checks Manage Roles for channel permission edits. The existing bot-owned control message upgrades in place with a doodle cover, animated icons and 15 buttons: details, rename, capacity, lock/unlock, hide/show, screen sharing, invite, remove access, transfer, claim, disconnect, audio quality and confirmed deletion. Controls are private replies, bound to the interacting member and current room. Owners do not receive server-wide moderation permissions.

Invitations are targeted DMs to eligible current server members, expire after ten minutes and grant View/Connect only after recipient acceptance. No permanent server invite is generated and no invitee is moved automatically. Owner presence, current ownership, category access, membership screening, capacity and one-time state are checked again. Five invitations per ten-minute window and duplicate-recipient throttling limit unsolicited repeats. Declines and closed DMs grant no access. Removing access invalidates earlier pending invitations; a new accepted invitation may restore access.

Locks override ordinary role-level Connect allows; hide overrides ordinary role-level View allows. The current owner, approved guests and staff retain the intended access. Server Administrator bypasses channel permissions. Staff cannot be removed through owner controls. Speak/server mute restrictions are preserved. Screen-sharing controls manage Stream only. Bitrates respect the server boost tier. Names are bounded and renames limited to two per ten-minute window; creation to three per five-minute window. New rooms start at 64 kbps.

A departed owner has a two-minute grace period before another eligible connected member may claim. Empty tracked rooms close after a short safety window. Transient cache misses or REST failures do not erase room ownership; only confirmed deletion/404 does. Departures revoke invitation/access grants and admissions recheck category eligibility, so stale member overwrites do not bypass server verification. Private room metadata and invitation IDs are persisted; invitation records and quotas are cleaned after one day. No DM contents or audio are recorded.

Regression checks cover category versus lobby inheritance, role-level locks, unlock restoration, preserved Speak denials, screen sharing, invitation guild/recipient/expiry/state binding and persistent throttling. Live human microphone, ownership, invite-DM and room-deletion interaction paths require member testing; automated deployment never moves or messages test members.
