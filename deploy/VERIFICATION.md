# Verification and account screening

`/verify action:Setup channel:#verify-here role:@Legacy confirm_access:true review_channel:#staff-reports` posts a bot-owned panel. Defaults: seven-day account-age review threshold and ten-minute join wait. Adjust with `min_account_days` (1–365) and `join_wait_minutes` (0–1440). Setup requires Manage Server and Manage Roles, destination access, Embed Links/Attach Files, a zero-global-permission whitelist role below the bot and configuring staff, and a private review channel. Server Members intent and ENABLE_MEMBER_EVENTS=true are required. Existing verified member roles are preserved.

Members complete a random six-character image challenge in a private interaction, using an Enter code modal. Challenges expire after two minutes, allow three wrong attempts, and can be started five times per ten-minute fixed window. Each is bound to the member, guild, current policy and join session, stored as a salted SHA-256 digest and consumed atomically once. Staff help offers an accessible manual review alternative. Discord native membership screening must be completed first, including before a staff approval.

Accounts younger than the threshold, three observed joins within 24 hours, or an observed arrival surge of eight members within two minutes require private staff review. No automatic ban or kick is applied. Discord does not provide account-linking or IP data: these are risk signals, not reliable detection or proof of an alternate account. The visual code provides friction, not a sophisticated CAPTCHA or identity verification. Only joins observed while the bot is running contribute to rejoin/surge signals.

Review panels offer Approve/Decline. `/verify action:Status` lists pending requests; `/verify action:Approve member:@member reason:...` and Deny support manual review. Decisions require Manage Server AND Manage Roles, role hierarchy and private review-channel visibility/history access. Approval rechecks current membership, rules screening, join session, role safety and join wait. Concurrent approvals are claimed once; denial does not remove roles or ban. Failed grants return to pending; interrupted claims recover after five minutes.

The verification role cannot be obtained through public self-role commands/panels or autoroles. Settings restores cannot expose it that way. Trusted staff can still explicitly assign roles using Discord or staff role commands. Restrict access using channel overwrites for the whitelist role; verification does not rewrite server channel permissions. Existing old panels use the new checks; after Setup only the explicitly bound newest panel is accepted.

SQLite retention: expired challenges are removed by the minute worker; quota records and observed joins expire after one day; verification requests expire after 30 days. Records contain Discord IDs, join/account timestamps, screening reasons and review state, never IP addresses, emails, phone numbers, passwords or OAuth tokens. Staff notifications and moderation case receipts follow the server's existing Discord/audit retention; deleting the transient review record does not erase those copies. Challenges and review policy are intentionally excluded from general settings backups. Keep staff review channels private.

`deploy/verification-upgrade.py` can conservatively bind/refresh a known existing bot panel on the VPS. Run without --apply to inspect. It validates the role, existing bot-owned panel and private review audience, never changes member assignments, and refuses to overwrite an explicit policy. Stop the bot during application and restart to reload settings. If the HTTP edit fails after the database commit, the old Verify button remains protected; use /verify Setup to publish a fresh panel.


After a successful staff approval and role grant, the member receives one branded approval DM with a generated card, animated check icon and server link. Staff notes, screening reasons and private review-channel IDs are not copied into that DM. A blocked DM never reverses access; the staff response and review embed explicitly show delivery status. Approval DM receipt IDs are stored in outgoing_dms for existing /dm-clear handling; content is not stored. The approval state is claimed once, so duplicate clicks do not resend. Previously approved members are not mass-messaged. Card-render failure falls back to the complete accessible text embed. Live verification panel artwork/icons refresh on startup without changing its policy or role assignments.
